Plotting

The plotting functions are declared in src/plotting.jl and have their methods in the package extension VlasovMethodsMakieExt, which loading CairoMakie activates:

using VlasovMethods, CairoMakie

Each function returns a Figure and writes no file. A driver in scripts/ calls these functions and saves each Figure under a name of its own. The figures of the papers are drawn by them, and the examples of this manual use them too.

Distributions and series

functiondraws
plot_distributiona grid distribution as a heatmap, or the weighted histogram of 1D1V particles
plot_particlesthe particles of a distribution or of a saved state of Snapshots as a scatter
plot_seriesseveral series against time on one axis
plot_groupsgroups of series, one colour per group
plot_dampingthe peaks of $\log \lvert E_1 \rvert$ of damping runs with their fitted rates
plot_phase_spacethe particles of a trajectory at several time slices, one panel each
plot_velocity_projectionsthe right-hand side of a collision model and the spline projections of the particles

The collision figures

functiondraws
plot_collision_distributionthe initial and the final distribution, with the Maxwellian of the initial moments
plot_driftthe relative drift of the conserved quantities, on a logarithmic axis
plot_entropythe entropy $S_h$ against time
plot_errorthe error of a run against a closed form, and the closed form
plot_convergencean error against the cell width, with the fitted power law
plot_costthe error of each run against its wall-clock cost

Conventions of the logarithmic axes

A quantity that is conserved exactly has a relative drift of zero, which a logarithmic axis cannot show. plot_drift, plot_error and plot_cost draw such a line one decade below the smallest positive value on the axis, and drop the points where a time or a value is not finite. An axis on which every value is the same spans a decade on each side of it. Each function throws an ArgumentError where nothing finite is left to draw.
