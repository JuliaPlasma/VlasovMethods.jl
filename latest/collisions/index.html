The figures of the collision manuscripts

Each figure on this page has one driver in scripts/ and one command that makes it. A driver reads its flags through scripts/options.jl, defaults to the manuscript's parameters, writes its figures into --results-dir and one HDF5 file per run into --runs-dir, and prints its wall-clock time, the package loads included. Each HDF5 file carries the attribute commit, the git rev-parse HEAD of the checkout or "unknown" outside one, the attribute command, the driver's arguments quoted for the shell by Base.shell_escape, the attribute script, the driver's file name, and one attribute per option. A directory that does not exist is created.

A driver loads CairoMakie and draws each figure with one of the collision figures' plotting functions, which the page Plotting documents; it builds no figure itself.

The flag --quick replaces some of the manuscript's parameters with smaller ones. scripts/gate.jl runs every driver with it, in a process of its own and into a temporary directory, and checks that each exits 0 within 900 s, writes its figures and its HDF5 files, and records commit, command and the parameters of --quick:

julia --startup-file=no --project=scripts scripts/gate.jl

Particle discretisation of the Lenard–Bernstein operator

The drivers scripts/pd_lb_*.jl make the eleven figures of the Lenard–Bernstein manuscript (PD-LB). They share scripts/pd_lb_common.jl. Every spline is cubic, on the velocity domain [−10, 10], and M, the flag --nbasis, is the number of its basis functions: a cubic basis on ncell cells has ncell + 3 of them, so the manuscript's "41 elements" are 41 basis functions on 38 cells. Where a projection is not positive at a particle, or a particle leaves the velocity domain, a run stops with the model's DomainError: the driver writes the HDF5 file of that run with the attribute stopped_at, the time of the step that threw, and the times t and the velocities z of the steps reached, writes no figure for that run, and exits non-zero. The sweeps of figures 1 and 2 are the exception: a point of a sweep where the operator stops is left out of its figure, and the driver exits 0. A run to the time t with the step h takes t / h steps, rounded up, so the quick run of figures 3 to 6 takes 63 steps of 8e-4 and ends at t = 0.0504. K94 of KNOWN_ISSUES.md records each stop at the manuscript's parameters.

A sweep that computes fewer than two points leaves plot_convergence nothing to fit, and its ArgumentError stops the driver.

Metriplectic particle methods for the Lenard–Bernstein operator

The driver scripts/lenard_bernstein_metriplectic.jl runs the three tests of the metriplectic Lenard–Bernstein manuscript (MP-LB) under MetriplecticLenardBernstein, with the parameters of the PD-LB figures of the same distribution, since MP-LB states none: 41 cubic basis functions on [−10, 10] and ν = 1 throughout, and

  • --case shifted-normal, the default: 1000 velocities from the normal distribution of mean 2 and variance 1, h = 8e-4, to t = 1, as figures 3 to 5;
  • --case double-maxwellian: 1000 velocities, half from the normal distribution of mean 2 and half from that of mean −2, h = 8e-4, to t = 5, seeded with --seed 1, as figures 7 and 8;
  • --case uniform: 200 velocities from the uniform distribution on [−2, 2], h = 1e-4, to t = 1, seeded with --seed 1, as figures 9 and 10.

--bracket takes dougherty, the default, for DoughertyBracket, the bracket of MP-LB, or jeyakumar for JeyakumarBracket. A case or a bracket that is not one of these stops the driver with an ArgumentError that names the flag and the value, before any run. Each step is the model's implicit midpoint step, solved by Picard_iterate_over_particles!.

A run writes lenard_bernstein_metriplectic_<case>_<bracket>.hdf5 and three figures with the same stem: _initial_final_distribution.pdf, _conservation.pdf, the relative drift of the mass, the momentum and the energy, and _entropy_log.pdf, the relative change |S_h(t) − S_h(0)| / |S_h(0)| of the entropy on a logarithmic axis. A step that throws, the DomainError of the projection or the error of a Picard solve that does not converge, stops the run as a single run of PD-LB stops: the HDF5 file carries stopped_at, the steps reached and, with the shifted normal, the times and temperatures of the rate check, no figure is written, and the driver exits non-zero.

The rate check

With the shifted normal, the driver first runs the rate check of its bracket. The bracket's density n₀ and mean u₀ are frozen at those of the initial particles and its temperature at T₀ = 2, the coefficients of the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck limit. The particles are scaled about their mean to the temperature T₀ / 2 = 1, from which they relax to T₀. In the continuum limit, where S_γ / w_γ is ∂ᵥ log f, the Dougherty form is v̇ = −ν T₀ ∂ᵥ log f − ν (v − u₀), and the Jeyakumar form is the same times n₀ / T₀, so the temperature relaxes to T₀ as exp(−λ t) with

\[\lambda_\mathrm{Dougherty} = 2 \nu , \qquad \lambda_\mathrm{Jeyakumar} = \frac{2 \nu n_0}{T_0} = \nu n_0 .\]

The Jeyakumar rate carries the temperature and the Dougherty rate does not. The weights of the particles sum to n₀ = 1, so at T₀ = 2 the two closed forms differ by two; at a temperature near n₀, such as the unit variance of the sample, they would be equal up to its sampling error. The driver fits λ to ln(T₀ − T(t)) over the run, stops with an ArgumentError where a temperature reaches or crosses T₀, and exits non-zero where the fit differs from the closed form of its bracket by more than --tolerance, 5 %, or from the control, the closed form of the other bracket, by less. Only the shifted normal takes --tolerance. With the parameters above:

bracketfitted λclosed formrelative differencecontrolcontrol's relative difference
Dougherty1.9891152ν = 2.0000005.4e-3ν n₀ = 1.0000009.9e-1
Jeyakumar0.993993ν n₀ = 1.0000006.0e-32ν = 2.0000005.0e-1

Each step of the check solves the implicit midpoint rule by a fixed-point iteration damped by 1/2. Where a particle in a sparse tail sits where f_s is small, the step is stiff and the iteration stalls; after 1000 iterations the step takes the last iterate, and the driver prints how many steps did so: 1 of the 1250 for the Dougherty form and 2 for the Jeyakumar form, at a largest residual of 3.1e-3 and 2.1e-4. The quick run, 200 particles to t = 0.05, fits 1.969792 against 2 and 0.998385 against 1, within 1.5 % and 0.16 %, with controls 97 % and 50 % away, and no step at the cap. With --quick --npart 2000 the fits are 1.999774 and 1.000624, with controls 100 % and 50 % away.

The check runs on the shifted normal only, the default case, because the closed form is not met at the stated tolerance on every case. With the same scaling, the same frozen T₀ = 2 and the rate check's solver, the fits differ from their closed forms by:

Particle methods for the Landau operator

The Landau manuscript (MP-L) states no numerical parameters, so the two Landau scripts run the three 2D reference cases of the package, AnisotropicBiMaxwellian, DoubleMaxwellian2D and BKWDistribution, at parameters of their own. Both take the kernel |u|^{γ+2} (I − û ⊗ û) at ν = 1, with γ = −3, the Coulomb case, by default, and quadratic splines in two velocity dimensions, M = (ncell + 2)² basis functions on ncell cells per axis.

The three cases

scripts/landau_cases.jl runs one case under one method. --method has no default: pairwise takes PairwiseLandau with discrete_gradient_step! and the step h = 1e-5, and spline takes Landau, the appendix method, with Picard_iterate_Landau_nls! and h = 1e-8; each step is solved to the absolute residual 1e-10. --case takes

  • bimaxwellian, the default: case (a), AnisotropicBiMaxwellian(0.5, 0.3);
  • double-maxwellian: case (b), DoubleMaxwellian2D of two halves of the variances (0.5, 0.5) and the means (1, 0) and (−1, 0);
  • bkw: case (c), BKWDistribution(0.2), the BKW profile at t₀ = 0.2.

Each draws 1000 particles of the weight 1/1000 from --seed 20261107 and takes 100 steps on 8 cells per axis of [−L, L]², with L 1.5 times the largest velocity component of the sample. A method or a case that is not one of these stops the script with an ArgumentError that names the flag and the value, and a missing method with one that names the flag, before any directory is created.

A run writes landau_<case>_<method>_gamma<γ>.hdf5 with the times t, the velocities v, the mass, the momentum, the energy and the entropy S_h, and two figures with the same stem: _conservation.pdf, the relative drift of the mass and the energy, and _entropy.pdf, S_h against t. The momentum of a sample is near zero, so the script prints its largest absolute drift. A step that throws, the DomainError of the projection or the error of a solve that does not converge, stops the run as a single run of PD-LB stops: the HDF5 file carries stopped_at and the steps reached, no figure is written, and the script exits non-zero.

The rate check

At the Maxwell molecules, γ = 0, the difference T₁₁ − T₂₂ of the second moments decays at the closed rate 8 at ν = 1 and density one, whatever the temperatures, which scripts/reference_forms.jl derives; --rate-check reads the rate from its function rate_checks. The check takes the 60 × 60 lattice of velocities on [−3.4, 3.4]², weighted by the bi-Maxwellian of the variances 0.5 and 0.3 and normalised to one, and 16 × 16 cells on [−3.5, 3.5]². The rate is instantaneous, λ = −(d gap/dt) / gap at t = 0 with gap = T₁₁ − T₂₂ and its derivative from collisional_vectorfield!, so it needs neither a step nor a seed. The script exits non-zero, after it has written its files, where λ differs from 8 by more than --tolerance, 2 %, or from the control, the same closed form with the constant 4, by less:

The comparison

scripts/landau_compare.jl evaluates the vector field of both methods at γ = 0 on the weighted lattice of the rate check with N = n² particles, --lattice 12,20,40,60, and on --ncell 4,8,15,16 cells, M = 36, 100, 289, 324. It measures (d gap/dt) / gap with its sign, its relative error against the closed form −8, and the smallest wall-clock time of three evaluations of collisional_vectorfield! after one that compiles. Each row states the rank condition of the appendix method, dN ≥ M − 1 with d = 2, which the appendix needs for K K⁺ = I of the stacked matrix K of M rows and dN columns, and whether every support of a basis function holds a particle, which it needs as well; a row where either fails is printed as measured. It writes landau_compare.md, landau_compare.hdf5, and the figures landau_compare_pairwise.pdf and landau_compare_spline.pdf of plot_cost, one marker per row of the table, every row drawn.
