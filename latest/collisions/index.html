The figures of the collision manuscripts

Each figure on this page has one driver in scripts/ and one command that makes it. A driver reads its flags through scripts/options.jl, defaults to the manuscript's parameters, writes its figures into --results-dir and one HDF5 file per run into --runs-dir, and prints its wall-clock time, the package loads included. Each HDF5 file carries the attribute commit, the git rev-parse HEAD of the checkout or "unknown" outside one, the attribute command, the driver's arguments joined by spaces, the attribute script, the driver's file name, and one attribute per option. A directory that does not exist is created.

A driver loads CairoMakie and draws each figure with one of the collision figures' plotting functions, which the page Plotting documents; it builds no figure itself.

The flag --quick replaces some of the manuscript's parameters with smaller ones. scripts/gate.jl runs every driver with it, in a process of its own and into a temporary directory, and checks that each exits 0 within 240 s, writes its figures and its HDF5 files, and records commit, command and the parameters of --quick:

julia --startup-file=no --project=scripts scripts/gate.jl

The measured times below are of one run each on an Apple M4 Max with Julia 1.13.3, one driver at a time.

Particle discretisation of the Lenard–Bernstein operator

The drivers scripts/pd_lb_*.jl make the eleven figures of the Lenard–Bernstein manuscript (PD-LB). They share scripts/pd_lb_common.jl. Every spline is cubic, on the velocity domain [−10, 10], and M, the flag --nbasis, is the number of its basis functions: a cubic basis on ncell cells has ncell + 3 of them, so the manuscript's "41 elements" are 41 basis functions on 38 cells. Where a projection is not positive at a particle, or a particle leaves the velocity domain, a run stops with the model's DomainError: the driver writes the HDF5 file of that run with the attribute stopped_at, the time of the step that threw, and the times t and the velocities z of the steps reached, writes no figure for that run, and exits non-zero. The sweeps of figures 1 and 2 are the exception: a point of a sweep where the operator stops is left out of its figure, and the driver exits 0. A run to the time t with the step h takes t / h steps, rounded up, so the quick run of figures 3 to 6 takes 63 steps of 8e-4 and ends at t = 0.0504. K94 of KNOWN_ISSUES.md records each stop at the manuscript's parameters.

Figures 1 and 2

julia --project=scripts scripts/pd_lb_fig1_2.jl

Figure 1 is the norm ‖v̇‖₂ of the right-hand side at 10⁵ normal velocities, which are never projected, with the Maxwellian projected onto M = 24, …, 78 basis functions, against the cell width. Figure 2 is the sample variance Σ v̇² / N of N projected normal velocities on 41 basis functions, against 1 / N, for N from 10² to 10⁵ in steps of a quarter decade. Files: pd_lb_fig1.pdf, pd_lb_fig2.pdf, pd_lb_fig1.hdf5, pd_lb_fig2.hdf5.

Measured: 24.0 s, and the driver exits 0. Where the projection is not positive at a particle, the DomainError of compute_coefficients, the operator stops at that point of the sweep: the driver prints the stop, leaves the point out of the figure and goes on, and the HDF5 file of the figure records the first such M or N as the attribute stopped_at beside the points it computed. With the defaults, figure 1 stops at M = 25, where the projected Maxwellian is −2.4e-5 at two of the 10⁵ velocities, and draws the other 54 values of M; figure 2 stops at N = 5623 and N = 31623, and draws the other 11 values of N. A sweep that computes fewer than two points leaves plot_convergence nothing to fit, and its ArgumentError stops the driver.

--quick takes 2000 velocities, M ∈ {24, 41, 78} and N from 100 to 1778.

Figures 3 to 6

julia --project=scripts scripts/pd_lb_fig3_6.jl

The relaxation of the normal distribution of mean 2 under ConservativeLenardBernstein and its implicit midpoint rule: 1000 particles, 41 basis functions, h = 8e-4, ν = 1, to t = 1. Figure 3 is the initial and the final distribution, figure 4 the relative drift of the mass, the momentum and the energy, figure 5 the entropy S_h, and figure 6 the initial and the final distribution of the same run with 200 particles. Files: pd_lb_fig3.pdf to pd_lb_fig6.pdf, pd_lb_fig3_5.hdf5, pd_lb_fig6.hdf5.

Measured: 381.4 s, and the driver exits 0.

--quick takes 200 particles and t = 0.05.

Figures 7 and 8

julia --project=scripts scripts/pd_lb_fig7_8.jl

The relaxation of the bi-Maxwellian of the two normal distributions of mean ±2 under ConservativeLenardBernstein: 1000 particles, h = 8e-4, to t = 5, the random number generator seeded with --seed 1. Figure 7 is the initial and the final distribution, and figure 8 the entropy. Files: pd_lb_fig7.pdf, pd_lb_fig8.pdf, pd_lb_fig7_8.hdf5.

The run with the manuscript's parameters, 6250 steps, stops after 289 s, in the step 1768 to t = 1.4144: the projection of the particles is -1.58e-7 at v = -6.42, and the conservative operator, which requires f_s > 0, throws the DomainError of compute_coefficients. As the rule above says for a single run, the driver writes pd_lb_fig7_8.hdf5 with stopped_at = 1.4144 and the 1767 steps reached, writes no figure and exits non-zero; K94 records the stop.

--quick takes 200 particles and t = 0.2.

Figures 9 and 10

julia --project=scripts scripts/pd_lb_fig9_10.jl

The relaxation of the uniform distribution on [−2, 2] under ConservativeLenardBernstein: 200 particles, h = 1e-4, to t = 1, seeded with --seed 1. Figure 9 is the initial and the final distribution, and figure 10 the entropy. Files: pd_lb_fig9.pdf, pd_lb_fig10.pdf, pd_lb_fig9_10.hdf5.

Measured: 379.7 s, while other Julia processes ran on the machine, and the driver exits 0.

--quick takes h = 1e-3 and t = 0.05.

Figure 11

julia --project=scripts scripts/pd_lb_fig11.jl

The cumulant appendix fixes the coefficients at A₁ = 0 and A₂ = 1, which is the right-hand side of LenardBernstein, and runs it with the implicit midpoint scheme of the time discretisation, which the driver uses in place of the model's own RK438. The bi-Maxwellian of figure 7 with 1000 particles, 41 basis functions, h = 5e-3, to t = 1, seeded with --seed 1. The closed form of the appendix is κ₄(t) = κ₄(0) exp(−4 ν t) at the equilibrium variance 1 / A₂ = 1. The driver prints the relative error of the particles' fourth cumulant against it at the first three steps, and exits non-zero where one exceeds --tolerance, 5 %. Figure 11 is the error and the closed form, both relative to κ₄(0). Files: pd_lb_fig11.pdf, pd_lb_fig11.hdf5. With --seed 1234, the implicit midpoint step sends a particle out of the velocity domain at t = 0.035, and the run stops (K94 of KNOWN_ISSUES.md).

Measured: 79.0 s, and the driver exits 0. The three relative errors are 8.4e-4, 1.5e-3 and 1.8e-3.

--quick takes 200 particles, h = 1e-2 and t = 0.1.
