Dynamical low rank

The low-rank distribution and the grid factor space

VlasovMethods.AbstractFactorSpace — Type
AbstractFactorSpace{DT}

The supertype of the spaces that hold the factors of a LowRankDistribution, with the element type DT. A concrete space defines size(space) = (nx, nv), the lengths of the x and the v factors, and extends the four functions of its weighted inner products: inner_x, inner_v, orthonormalize_x and orthonormalize_v. eltype(space) is DT.

source
VlasovMethods.GridFactorSpace — Type
GridFactorSpace(x, v)
GridFactorSpace(dist::GridDistribution)

The factor space of the nodes of a GridDistribution: the periodic x grid x and the bounded v grid v, two ranges of one element type DT. x holds at least one node and v at least two. size(space) is (length(x), length(v)).

The inner products are the rectangle rules of the grid, with $h_x =$ step(x) and $h_v =$ step(v):

\[\langle a, b \rangle_x = h_x \sum_i a_i b_i , \qquad \langle a, b \rangle_v = h_v \sum_j a_j b_j ,\]

so the rule in v is the rule of velocity_moments of a GridDistribution. VlasovMethods.inner_x and VlasovMethods.inner_v evaluate them, and VlasovMethods.orthonormalize_x and VlasovMethods.orthonormalize_v orthonormalise in them by the Householder QR of LinearAlgebra.qr.

VlasovMethods.orthonormalize_v — Method
orthonormalize_v(space::GridFactorSpace, A)

(Q, R) with Q = Q̃ / √h_v and R = R̃ from the thin Householder QR √h_v A = Q̃ R̃, for A of size nv × k: A = Q R, h_v QᵀQ = I, Q of size nv × k and R of size k × k. Throws an ArgumentError for k > nv, and a DimensionMismatch for another number of rows than nv. The entries of A are not checked: a non-finite entry propagates into Q and R.

VlasovMethods.LowRankDistribution — Type
LowRankDistribution{DT, ST, XT, SMT, VT}

A distribution function of a 1D1V phase space in the factored form

\[f \approx X S V^\mathsf{T} ,\]

a DistributionFunction{DT, 1, 1}. Its fields:

  • space, the factor space, a concrete subtype of VlasovMethods.AbstractFactorSpace{DT}, with nx, nv = size(space);
  • X, the nx × r matrix of the x factors;
  • S, the r × r coefficient matrix, so the rank is r = size(S, 1);
  • V, the nv × r matrix of the v factors;
  • nfixed, the number of leading columns of V that span the fixed velocity modes.

The type is mutable: a step may assign to X, S, V and nfixed factors of another rank of the same array types. The assignment is not checked against the sizes. size(d) is size(d.space) and eltype(d) is DT.

LowRankDistribution(space, X, S, V; nfixed = 0)

Wrap the factors X, S and V, of the element type DT of space, without a copy. Throws a DimensionMismatch for factors of other sizes, and an ArgumentError for a rank r outside 1:min(nx, nv) or nfixed outside 0:r. It does not check that X and V are orthonormal: that is the caller's contract.

LowRankDistribution(dist::GridDistribution, r; fixed)

Factorise the node values f = dist.values at the rank r on GridFactorSpace(dist). fixed is an nv × m matrix of velocity modes, converted to the element type of dist, none by default. With $h_x$, $h_v$ the steps of the grid:

  1. the fixed modes are orthonormalised first, by the weighted QR of fixed, so the first k columns of V span the first k columns of fixed, and nfixed = m;
  2. the next q columns of V span the leading right singular vectors of $\sqrt{h_x h_v}\, g$, with $g = f - h_v f V_m V_m^\mathsf{T}$ the part of f outside the fixed modes $V_m$, where q is the smaller of r - m and the number of singular values above $\max(n_x, n_v)\, \epsilon \, \| \sqrt{h_x h_v} f \|_F$, with $\epsilon$ = eps(DT);
  3. the columns m + q + 1 to r complete V deterministically, from the full orthogonal factor of the Householder QR of $\sqrt{h_v} [V_m \; V_q]$, with $V_q$ the q singular vectors of item 2, divided by $\sqrt{h_v}$; no random numbers are drawn;
  4. X, S = orthonormalize_x(space, h_v f V).

So $X S V^\mathsf{T} = h_v f V V^\mathsf{T}$ is the weighted orthogonal projection of f onto the span of V, with $h_x X^\mathsf{T} X = I$ and $h_v V^\mathsf{T} V = I$. Without fixed modes it is the weighted truncated SVD of rank r; with m fixed modes it keeps every moment of f that the fixed modes span, such as the density, the momentum and the energy for the modes [1 v v²].

Throws an ArgumentError for r outside 1:min(nx, nv), for m > r, for linearly dependent fixed modes, and for a non-finite entry of f or of fixed; and a DimensionMismatch for fixed of another number of rows than nv.

The factored fields

VlasovMethods.LowRankFields1D1V — Type
LowRankFields1D1V(arakawa::Arakawa{T}, G::AbstractMatrix{T}, v::AbstractVector)
LowRankFields1D1V(space::GridFactorSpace, G)
LowRankFields1D1V(dist::LowRankDistribution, G)

The workspace of the K-, L- and S-fields of 1D1V Vlasov–Poisson on an nx × nv grid, in the element type T: the bracket arakawa, the $n_x \times n_x$ Green's matrix G of the x-grid, as _green_matrix builds it, and the kinetic energy kinetic = collect(v) .^ 2 ./ 2 of the velocity nodes v, in T. The fields are those of the full-order right-hand side _grid_1d1v_rhs! on the same bracket, G and nodes.

The first method throws a DimensionMismatch when size(G) != (nx, nx) or length(v) != nv. The second builds the bracket Arakawa(nx, nv, step(space.x), step(space.v)) of the grid of space, with (nx, nv) = size(space), and takes the nodes space.v. The third is the second on the space of dist, so it throws a MethodError for a distribution on a space other than a GridFactorSpace, because the stencil and its weights belong to the grid.

The functions _kfield!, _lfield!, _sfield! and _potential! take the workspace.

VlasovMethods._kfield! — Method
_kfield!(K̇, K, V, w::LowRankFields1D1V)
_kfield!(K̇, K, V, w::LowRankFields1D1V, ϕ)

Write the K-field of 1D1V Vlasov–Poisson into K̇ and return it:

\[\dot{K} = h_v \, F(K V^\mathsf{T}) \, V , \qquad F(f) = [h, f] , \qquad h = \phi \otimes 1 + 1 \otimes \tfrac{v^2}{2} ,\]

where $F$ is the right-hand side of _grid_1d1v_rhs! on the bracket, G and the velocity nodes of the workspace w, K is nx × r, V is nv × r, and K̇ is nx × r. Without ϕ the potential is that of $f = K V^\mathsf{T}$, from _potential!; with ϕ, a Vector of length nx, the potential is the given ϕ, frozen. The factors are Matrixes of the element type T of w; V need not be orthonormal.

The field is evaluated on the factors, without the $n_x \times n_v$ array: $h$ has rank 2, so each term of Arakawa's stencil projects onto V through $r \times r$ matrices of shifted columns, at a cost of $O((n_x + n_v) r^2)$ beyond the product with G.

Throws a DimensionMismatch before any write when the sizes do not fit the grid of w and the rank r = size(K, 2), and an ArgumentError when K̇ shares memory with K, V or ϕ. At r = 0 it returns the empty K̇. A non-finite entry propagates to the result, unchecked.

source
VlasovMethods._lfield! — Method
_lfield!(L̇, X, L, w::LowRankFields1D1V)
_lfield!(L̇, X, L, w::LowRankFields1D1V, ϕ)

Write the L-field of 1D1V Vlasov–Poisson into L̇ and return it:

\[\dot{L} = h_x \, F(X L^\mathsf{T})^\mathsf{T} X ,\]

with $F$ as in _kfield!, X of size nx × r, and L and L̇ of size nv × r. Without ϕ the potential is that of $f = X L^\mathsf{T}$, from _potential!(ϕ, X, L, w); with ϕ, the potential is the given ϕ, frozen. X need not be orthonormal. The evaluation, the edges and the errors are those of _kfield!, with L̇ in place of K̇ and the rank r = size(L, 2).

VlasovMethods._potential! — Method
_potential!(ϕ, K, V, w::LowRankFields1D1V)
_potential!(ϕ, X, S, V, w::LowRankFields1D1V)

Write the potential $\phi = G \rho$ of factored data into the Vector ϕ of length nx and return it, with G of the workspace w and the density

\[\rho = h_v K (V^\mathsf{T} 1) \quad \text{of } f = K V^\mathsf{T} , \qquad \rho = h_v X S (V^\mathsf{T} 1) \quad \text{of } f = X S V^\mathsf{T} ,\]

the density $\rho_i = h_v \sum_j f_{ij}$ of _grid_1d1v_rhs!, without the $n_x \times n_v$ array. The potential of $f = X L^\mathsf{T}$ is _potential!(ϕ, X, L, w). The factors are Matrixes of the element type T of w.

Throws a DimensionMismatch before any write when the sizes do not fit the grid of w and the rank, and an ArgumentError when ϕ shares memory with a factor. At the rank 0 it writes zeros. A non-finite entry propagates to the result, unchecked.

VlasovMethods._sfield! — Method
_sfield!(Ṡ, X, S, V, w::LowRankFields1D1V)
_sfield!(Ṡ, X, S, V, w::LowRankFields1D1V, ϕ)

Write the S-field of 1D1V Vlasov–Poisson into Ṡ and return it:

\[\dot{S} = h_x h_v \, X^\mathsf{T} F(X S V^\mathsf{T}) \, V ,\]

with $F$ as in _kfield!, X of size nx × r, V of size nv × r, and S and Ṡ of size r × r. Without ϕ the potential is that of $f = X S V^\mathsf{T}$, from _potential!(ϕ, X, S, V, w); with ϕ, the potential is the given ϕ, frozen. X and V need not be orthonormal. At r = 1 the field vanishes, up to round-off, by the antisymmetry of Arakawa's form. The evaluation, the edges and the errors are those of _kfield!, with Ṡ in place of K̇ and the rank r = size(X, 2).
