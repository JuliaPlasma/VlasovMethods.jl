Dynamical low rank

The low-rank distribution and the grid factor space

VlasovMethods.AbstractFactorSpace — Type
AbstractFactorSpace{DT}

The supertype of the spaces that hold the factors of a LowRankDistribution, with the element type DT. A concrete space defines size(space) = (nx, nv), the lengths of the x and the v factors, and extends the four functions of its weighted inner products: inner_x, inner_v, orthonormalize_x and orthonormalize_v. eltype(space) is DT.

source
VlasovMethods.GridFactorSpace — Type
GridFactorSpace(x, v)
GridFactorSpace(dist::GridDistribution)

The factor space of the nodes of a GridDistribution: the periodic x grid x and the bounded v grid v, two ranges of one element type DT. x holds at least one node and v at least two. size(space) is (length(x), length(v)).

The inner products are the rectangle rules of the grid, with $h_x =$ step(x) and $h_v =$ step(v):

\[\langle a, b \rangle_x = h_x \sum_i a_i b_i , \qquad \langle a, b \rangle_v = h_v \sum_j a_j b_j ,\]

so the rule in v is the rule of velocity_moments of a GridDistribution. VlasovMethods.inner_x and VlasovMethods.inner_v evaluate them, and VlasovMethods.orthonormalize_x and VlasovMethods.orthonormalize_v orthonormalise in them by the Householder QR of LinearAlgebra.qr.

VlasovMethods.orthonormalize_v — Method
orthonormalize_v(space::GridFactorSpace, A)

(Q, R) with Q = Q̃ / √h_v and R = R̃ from the thin Householder QR √h_v A = Q̃ R̃, for A of size nv × k: A = Q R, h_v QᵀQ = I, Q of size nv × k and R of size k × k. Throws an ArgumentError for k > nv, and a DimensionMismatch for another number of rows than nv. The entries of A are not checked: a non-finite entry propagates into Q and R.

VlasovMethods.LowRankDistribution — Type
LowRankDistribution{DT, ST, XT, SMT, VT}

A distribution function of a 1D1V phase space in the factored form

\[f \approx X S V^\mathsf{T} ,\]

a DistributionFunction{DT, 1, 1}. Its fields:

  • space, the factor space, a concrete subtype of VlasovMethods.AbstractFactorSpace{DT}, with nx, nv = size(space);
  • X, the nx × r matrix of the x factors;
  • S, the r × r coefficient matrix, so the rank is r = size(S, 1);
  • V, the nv × r matrix of the v factors;
  • nfixed, the number of leading columns of V that span the fixed velocity modes.

The type is mutable: a step may assign to X, S, V and nfixed factors of another rank of the same array types. The assignment is not checked against the sizes. size(d) is size(d.space) and eltype(d) is DT.

LowRankDistribution(space, X, S, V; nfixed = 0)

Wrap the factors X, S and V, of the element type DT of space, without a copy. Throws a DimensionMismatch for factors of other sizes, and an ArgumentError for a rank r outside 1:min(nx, nv) or nfixed outside 0:r. It does not check that X and V are orthonormal: that is the caller's contract.

LowRankDistribution(dist::GridDistribution, r; fixed)

Factorise the node values f = dist.values at the rank r on GridFactorSpace(dist). fixed is an nv × m matrix of velocity modes, converted to the element type of dist, none by default. With $h_x$, $h_v$ the steps of the grid:

  1. the fixed modes are orthonormalised first, by the weighted QR of fixed, so the first k columns of V span the first k columns of fixed, and nfixed = m;
  2. the next q columns of V span the leading right singular vectors of $\sqrt{h_x h_v}\, g$, with $g = f - h_v f V_m V_m^\mathsf{T}$ the part of f outside the fixed modes $V_m$, where q is the smaller of r - m and the number of singular values above $\max(n_x, n_v)\, \epsilon \, \| \sqrt{h_x h_v} f \|_F$, with $\epsilon$ = eps(DT);
  3. the columns m + q + 1 to r complete V deterministically, from the full orthogonal factor of the Householder QR of $\sqrt{h_v} [V_m \; V_q]$, with $V_q$ the q singular vectors of item 2, divided by $\sqrt{h_v}$; no random numbers are drawn;
  4. X, S = orthonormalize_x(space, h_v f V).

So $X S V^\mathsf{T} = h_v f V V^\mathsf{T}$ is the weighted orthogonal projection of f onto the span of V, with $h_x X^\mathsf{T} X = I$ and $h_v V^\mathsf{T} V = I$. Without fixed modes it is the weighted truncated SVD of rank r; with m fixed modes it keeps every moment of f that the fixed modes span, such as the density, the momentum and the energy for the modes [1 v v²].

Throws an ArgumentError for r outside 1:min(nx, nv), for m > r, for linearly dependent fixed modes, and for a non-finite entry of f or of fixed; and a DimensionMismatch for fixed of another number of rows than nv.

SimpleSplines.evaluate — Method
evaluate(d::LowRankDistribution, x, v)

The bilinear interpolant of the grid values X S Vᵀ of d on a GridFactorSpace at (only(x), only(v)), with the index rule of the GridDistribution: periodic in x, zero outside the velocity range, and v at its upper end in the last cell. It interpolates two rows of X and two rows of V and contracts them with S, without the nx × nv array.

VlasovMethods.velocity_moments — Method
velocity_moments(d::LowRankDistribution)

The velocity moments (density, momentum, energy) of d on a GridFactorSpace, in the convention of velocity_moments of a GridDistribution, computed from the factors without the nx × nv array:

\[n = h_v X S (V^\mathsf{T} 1) , \qquad p = h_v X S (V^\mathsf{T} v) , \qquad \varepsilon = \tfrac{1}{2} h_v X S (V^\mathsf{T} v^2) .\]
