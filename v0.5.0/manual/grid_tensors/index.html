Grid-based reduced tensors

The grid Vlasov–Poisson model of Vlasov–Poisson is $\dot{f} = -[f, h]$ with the Arakawa bracket on an $n_x \times n_v$ grid. The bracket is bilinear, so it is a tensor of three indices over the grid nodes:

\[[f, h]_I = \sum_{J, K} T_{IJK} \, f_J \, h_K .\]

The Hamiltonian splits into the kinetic part $v_j^2 / 2$, which does not depend on $f$, and the potential $\phi_i$, which depends on $x$ only. A Galerkin reduction approximates $f \approx \sum_j P_j a_j$ with a reduced basis $P$, and projects the equation onto the test basis. The tensors on this page are the reduced forms of $T$ that this reduction needs. They are lazy: an entry is summed when it is read, over the stencil of the bracket only, and nothing of the full size $(n_x n_v)^3$ is ever formed.

typeentrycontracts the third index with
PotentialReducedTensor(T, Pi, Pj, Pk)$\sum_{I,J,K} T_{IJK} P^i_{Ii} P^j_{Jj} P^k_{Kk}$a basis Pk of grid functions
VelocityReducedMatrix(T, Pi, Pj, v²)$\sum_{I,J,K} T_{IJK} P^i_{Ii} P^j_{Jj} \, v_{j(K)}^2 / 2$the kinetic energy
FullyReducedTensor(T, Pi, Pj, Px)$\sum_{I,J,K} T_{IJK} P^i_{Ii} P^j_{Jj} P^x_{i(K) k}$a basis Px of functions of $x$

The grid nodes are counted in the order of vec(dist.values) of a GridDistribution, the $x$ index fastest. bracket_tensor builds $T$ from the Arakawa bracket of GeometricBrackets:

using GeometricBrackets: Arakawa
using VlasovMethods

nx, nv = 4, 5
T = bracket_tensor(Arakawa(nx, nv, 0.5, 0.5))
size(T)
(20, 20, 20)

Two reduced bases of three and two columns give the reduced tensors:

P = [cos(π * (i + 2j) / (nx * nv)) for i in 1:(nx * nv), j in 1:3]
Px = [cos(2π * (i - 1) * k / nx) for i in 1:nx, k in 0:1]
v = range(-1.0, 1.0; length = nv)

(potential = size(PotentialReducedTensor(T, P, P, P)),
 velocity = size(VelocityReducedMatrix(T, P, P, collect(v) .^ 2)),
 fully = size(FullyReducedTensor(T, P, P, Px)))
(potential = (3, 3, 3), velocity = (3, 3), fully = (3, 3, 2))

The projections need one row per grid node, or one per $x$-node for Px, and the element type of the tensor. The constructors check both, and an $x$ or $v$ direction of fewer than three nodes, which the stencil of the bracket would visit twice.

Moments on the grid

velocity_moments computes the density, the momentum and the kinetic energy density of a GridDistribution at each $x$-node, by the rectangle rule in $v$. The stencils behind it, such as _apply_∫dv!, act on the state vector vec(dist.values) and write into a preallocated vector.

Collision tensors

The grid discretisation of a collision operator $C[f]$ that is at most quadratic in $f$ is a tensor of the same kind. The internal types QuadraticCollisions, CollisionTensor and ReducedCollisionTensor hold its stencil, the lazy tensor on the grid and its reduction. The internal functions _get_MC̃_quadratic and _get_MC̃_cubic assemble the Galerkin forms of $\rho^2 C[f]$. The cubic tensor conserves the mass, the momentum and the energy; the quadratic one conserves the mass and the energy only. Both are valid while $f$ vanishes at the ends of the velocity grid, because the velocity stencil wraps around. None of these is exported, and no model of the package uses them; the tests check their conservation.
